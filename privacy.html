Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Little Elsewhere Analytics is an application under development intended to help an authorized TikTok account holder review video performance and engagement. The application is not affiliated with TikTok.

Information we may process

Only after an account holder authorizes access, the application may process TikTok profile information, video identifiers and metadata, and metrics such as video views, likes, comments and shares, subject to the permissions granted and the availability of the TikTok API. During development, some of these features may not be active.

Purposes and legal basis

We use authorized account information to display analytics, compare video performance, and provide content-planning insights. Where applicable, processing is based on the account holder's authorization or another applicable legal basis.

Sharing and third-party services

TikTok supplies data when authorized. Hosting and technical providers may process data as needed to operate the service. No sale of TikTok account data is intended. AI services may be considered in future versions; their use and any sharing of TikTok data will be disclosed here before activation.

Storage, security and retention

During development, this static informational website does not itself request TikTok login details or retrieve TikTok analytics. If authenticated features are enabled, we will use access controls, store tokens securely outside public source code, and retain account data only as needed for the service or applicable obligations. Retention arrangements will be specified before launch.

Your choices and rights

You may revoke connected-app access in your TikTok settings. You can contact us to request information about access, correction, or deletion of personal data where applicable. We will respond in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

Changes

This policy will be updated before new data collection or integrations are activated, and material changes will be reflected on this page.
